## Detailed Troubleshooting Records

Detailed troubleshooting records should include:

### Problem

What went wrong?

### Symptoms

What did I observe?

### Evidence

What commands, screenshots or test results confirmed the problem?

### Hypotheses

What possible causes did I consider?

### Root Cause

What was the actual cause?

### Recovery

What action fixed the problem?

### Validation

How did I confirm that the problem was resolved?

### Prevention

What could prevent the problem from happening again?

### Lessons Learned

What did I learn from the incident?

## Day 1 — Mini Troubleshooting

### Problem
PC1 lost network connectivity after the cable between PC1 and Switch1 was removed.

### Symptoms
The physical connection between PC1 and Switch1 was unavailable.

### Evidence
The PC1-to-Switch1 link was disconnected in Packet Tracer.

### Hypotheses
The physical cable or connection between PC1 and Switch1 could be the cause.

### Root Cause
The cable between PC1 and Switch1 was intentionally removed.

### Recovery
Reconnected PC1 to Switch1 using the correct cable.

### Validation
The physical link was restored.

### Prevention
Verify physical connections when troubleshooting network connectivity problems.

### Lessons Learned
A physical-layer problem can cause a device to lose network connectivity. Checking the physical link is an important first troubleshooting step.
